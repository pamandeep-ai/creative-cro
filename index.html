  • Scale learnings

Revenue Potential

Creative + CRO together move more revenue than either alone.

Illustrative model based on a mid-size premium Shopify store. Not built from Royal Chess Mall's live GA/Shopify numbers — swap in actual sessions, CVR and AOV and the same math applies.

Baseline monthly revenue
₹20.9L
Base case (creative + CRO)
₹29.6L

+₹8.7L
Baseline Single lever Combined (creative + CRO)
Baseline ₹20.9L, Creative only ₹23.4L, CRO only ₹25.2L, Combined conservative ₹26.3L, Combined base case ₹29.6L, Combined aggressive ₹33.0L

Why they compound, not add: ad creative fixes the match between ad promise and landing page (+8–15% CVR), CRO fixes friction for whoever lands on the page (+12–25% CVR), and cart/checkout work lifts AOV separately (+4–10%). Run together, the effects multiply rather than stack.

Assumptions: 20,000 monthly sessions · 1.1% current CVR · ₹9,500 current AOV. The creative uplift is a hypothesis to A/B test, not a guaranteed result.
